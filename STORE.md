## Description (long)

**2,322 wallpapers. Every single one credited.**

Wiki Wallpapers 4D turns Wikimedia Commons — the free media library behind Wikipedia — into a wallpaper feed you can actually browse. No ads, no account, no tracking, no re-hosted images: every photo loads from Wikimedia's own servers and carries its photographer, licence and source page right on the card.

**A 4D liquid-glass interface**
Real refraction behind the glass panels, a 3D tilt and glare on every card, a parallax backdrop and gyroscope depth on phones. Blur, transparency, saturation, corner radius, tilt depth and animation speed are all adjustable, and your choices are remembered.

**Made for browsing**
61 categories, a mixed portrait/landscape masonry grid, instant search across titles, photographers and descriptions. The whole library is stored inside the app, so it opens instantly and works offline; new featured pictures stream in when you are online.

**Live from Wikimedia**
Pull down to refresh and the newest featured pictures appear at the top with a NEW badge. The app checks for new uploads every couple of minutes and offers them in a small snackbar — never interrupting what you are doing.

**Fullscreen viewer**
Edge-to-edge viewing with Fit and Fill, arrow keys or swipe to move between wallpapers, and a credit strip that stays visible so you always know whose photo you are looking at.

**Credits, everywhere**
Every card shows © photographer · licence. Every image window has a copy-ready credit line with a link to the licence and the Commons source page. The credits sheet lists all 2,322 images and 793 photographers, searchable, and exports to a .txt file.

**9 languages**
English, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Punjabi, Tamil and Telugu — the entire interface translates, while the wallpapers stay exactly the same.

**Honest about licences**
Most images are CC BY or CC BY-SA, which means credit is required — that is why the app never hides it. Some are public domain or CC0.

Open source (MIT) — the whole app is a single HTML file plus a thin Android WebView shell.
